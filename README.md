# Korean Self-Conscious Emotion Dictionary (KSED)

The KSED contains 328 Korean words associated with four self-conscious emotions: shame, guilt, authentic pride, and hubristic pride.

## Development

Candidate words were collected from dictionary definitions, psychological scales, and previous research, then expanded using related words. Researchers and independent raters evaluated their relevance. Frequency checks in books, news articles, and blog posts helped remove rarely used words. Word stems were prepared to capture different Korean word forms.

## Validation

- **Validation 1:** Examined emotion-word frequencies in essays about personal emotional experiences and evaluated emotion classification performance.
- **Validation 2:** Examined whether emotion-word frequencies differed as expected across reviews of films selected for their emotional themes. Film themes provide indirect evidence of the emotions expressed in individual reviews.

## Files

- **Korean Self-Conscious Emotion Dictionary.xlsx:** Korean words, English translations, and word stems.
- **Validation 1/** and **Validation 2/:** Files and analysis code used in each validation study.
